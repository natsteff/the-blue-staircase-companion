## Permanent visual identity
- Exact title: **The Blue Staircase**.
- Subtitle: **A Mystery in Playa del Carmen**.
- Front supporting line: **A mystery that helps you learn Spanish.** Keep clean and legible, with **no horizontal gold bars flanking this tagline**.
- Front scene: preserve prototype's lush **nighttime** courtyard, blue staircase and blue walking treads, **blue-and-white patterned tiles on the vertical risers**, lanterns, magenta bougainvillea, fountain, and ornate title hierarchy. **Night consistency:** sky should read as nighttime rather than bright dusk/daylight; lit lanterns, windows and reflections should supply localized warm lighting. Avoid making the staircase or courtyard so dark that the primary clue-setting is obscured.
- Back scene: **beach sunset only**; absolutely **no staircase** or hotel structure inserted on the beach.
- No visible author credit on cover. The publication metadata/front matter may retain author credit **Nate and OpenAI** as previously agreed.
- **Never** include ISBN, UPC, barcode, fake ISBN or fake barcode; these will not be issued.
- Keep consistent typography, color palette and visual identity across front and back without forcing the same background scene.
- The back synopsis remains spoiler-free and foregrounds Sally's Spanish-learning experience. Separate companion-app block explains **hear Spanish words pronounced**, **track vocabulary mastery**, **maintain an investigative case log**. Avoid claims of fully bilingual text or guaranteed learning outcomes.

## Edition treatment: reusable, temporary overlay
- **Working Draft** and **Chapters 1–4** are edition metadata, **not permanent cover text**.
- Preserve the navy-and-gold **metal plate / plaque** aesthetic for an unmistakable provisional badge; avoid loose plain text.
- Edition badge should be legible at e-reader thumbnail scale, distinct from subtitle/tagline, and placed consistently on front and back. Wording may be two lines: **CHAPTERS 1–4** / **WORKING DRAFT**.
- Production must support rendering both **clean permanent artwork** and **draft overlay variants** for chapter ranges (e.g., Chapters 1–2, 1–4, later ranges), and **final** editions without the plaque.
- Maintain reusable templates or layered source, not just flattened, text-baked images. Prefer automated edition labeling where feasible, so the range and draft status are a single source of truth.
